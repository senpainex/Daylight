Privacy, in plain language.

Daylight Pocket is published by the Daylight project. This policy describes the mobile companion and the providers a user chooses to connect.

Offline use

Without an account or AI provider, chat replies are generated on-device by the app's simple offline helper. Tasks are saved in this browser or app installation. Daylight does not send offline prompts to its operator.

Google Gemini and Claude

If you choose Gemini or Claude and enter your own API key, your question and recent in-memory conversation context are sent directly from your device to the selected provider after a confirmation prompt. The key is held in memory for the current page session and is not stored by Daylight. Provider terms, retention, availability, and charges are controlled by Google or Anthropic. Gemini Search grounding is optional and, when enabled, also sends the query to Google Search.

Conferbot

If you choose Conferbot and enter a bot ID, the app loads Conferbot's third-party widget script after consent. That script runs in the app page and may access page content and browser storage. Messages entered into its widget are handled by Conferbot under its own terms and privacy policy.

Sign-in and synced tasks

Google or phone sign-in is provided by Firebase when configured. Firebase processes sign-in identifiers; phone verification may send SMS and phone numbers may be processed for abuse prevention. When signed in, tasks sync to a Firestore document scoped to your Firebase user ID. When signed out, tasks stay local. The Daylight project does not receive provider API keys.

Downloads

The desktop app only downloads a direct HTTPS link after your confirmation. It saves the file to Downloads and does not run it.

Your choices

AI and account connections are optional. Sign out to stop account task sync. Clear this app's local storage to erase local tasks. Revoke API keys in the provider console and review/delete account data through the corresponding provider controls. For privacy questions, contact the Daylight repository owner through GitHub.
